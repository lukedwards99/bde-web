7 weeks post show and week 1 of the bulk. Weight has settled around 165 after all is said and done. I will be honest I didn’t quite meet my goal of preventing binging. However I did much better than last prep where I did 145-167 in a week and a half. The goal will be to do better after next prep. However, it is crazy the clarity you get once you get out of your lower set point for body fat. It is always insane to look back and see how ripped I was but remember at the time I thought I was still holding on to too much fat. The goal will be to slow roll this bulk now. I think I finally got my hunger in check. Additionally it is crazy the strength I am adding right now. All my lifts are up like 10%. Which is cool because I held onto most of my strength in prep so I am approaching the strongest I have ever been. I just have to be careful to let my body adapt to the heavier weights before pushing to much heavier. The goal is to add some weight to my lifts this offseason. Last off season was all about form and using better form as progressive overload. My form is now pretty close to perfect. So now it is time to add some weight.
