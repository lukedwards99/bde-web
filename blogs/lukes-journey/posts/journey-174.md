5 weeks post show. I think I finally cleared the post show dopamine and ghrelin dump that makes it difficult to not binge on every food in sight. It is  still difficult but my hunger is starting to return to normal levels. I really did have to dive bomb my calories at the end of prep and I had to pay for that. I have also been doing some introspection on my goals in bodybuilding which you can do a bit more clear eyed further from a show. I just won my first class at a show, it’s been about two years since I started bodybuilding, five years since I started lifting consistently 5 to 7 days a week. I am no longer just “competing in bodybuilding for fun” the next step is to attempt to become natural pro. Which I have always talked about as a goal but now it is a distinct possibility and well within my abilities. However that also present with a new set of challenges. If I am going to go for pro I am going to go all in, that is just my personality. However, I am also thinking “is this worth it” in terms of opportunity cost of doing other things with my life. For example I would like to go back to school and get my masters or even PhD. However it would be difficult to work full time, go to school and also be actively competing for my pro card. Like it’s possible but holy shit that’s a lot. I am leaning towards just full send it but I am kinda deathly afraid of burnout because of having burnt out hard in the past. Just some things I have been thinking about. My lifts in the gym are starting to go up a decent bit now after having plateaued during prep so that is also a lot of fun.
