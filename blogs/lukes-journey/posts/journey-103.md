“There is so much war and hatred in this world. Sometimes it seems like they are the only things that really exist. I want to do something about it. And I would really like to know, what the answer to finding true peace is.” - Jiraiya The Gallant (Naruto). Tomorrow will be the one year anniversary of this account. A year ago I was unsure if this would become anything. Now, looking back, this past year has been pretty good, great I may dare. Not just from a lifting standpoint, but a life standpoint as well. Thinking beyond this year, I think about how much this journey has given me. The gym has never been the cause or the solution to the problems I face day to day. But it is a constant activity that gives me great meaning. It is something that I can look forward to every day and it make me incredibly happy. Now it isn’t the only thing that gives me meaning, it is dangerous to get your source of meaning from one activity of ideology, but it is a major one (probably like #4 on the list). The gym has helped me self actualize my goals in life beyond what I am doing now. It has taught me to fill my life with the things I enjoy but it has also lead to a sober realization. Every day in the gym I meditate over all the hate I see in the world, and my own feelings about it. I want to do something about all this hate, but I haven’t found the solution yet. This helplessness I feel, to enact change, keeps me going, until I find a solution, or I pass on what I have found to the next generation. If have learned anything it is that nothing can conquer an indomitable human spirit that refuses to quit. I may never reach that level, but I will try.
